Thistlecombe Residents' Association

Notice to residents of Larkmere Court

The shared boiler serving Larkmere Court is due its annual inspection. The association can arrange the inspection for every flat in one visit, which keeps the cost at 38 per flat instead of the 95 a separate visit costs.

Consent form
Residents who want the association to arrange it return the signed consent form to the office by January 16.
Inspection
January 23, between 9am and 1pm. The engineer needs access to the airing cupboard in each flat on the list.
Not on the list
A flat that returns no form is not visited and arranges its own inspection.

Questions go to the association office during opening hours, Tuesday to Thursday, 10am to 2pm.

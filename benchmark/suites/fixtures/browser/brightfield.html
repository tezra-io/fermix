Brightfield Founders Fellowship

Spring cohort: how to apply

Brightfield backs first-time founders building a company in its first year. Each fellow receives a 24,000 stipend over six months, a desk at the Ostler Lane studio, and a weekly session with a partner.

Applications close
March 12, at 5pm. Late applications are not read.
Interviews
Shortlisted founders are invited to interview during the two weeks after the round closes.
Cohort starts
The first Monday of May.

What we look for in founders

  • You have talked to the people you are building for, and can say what you learned.
  • You ship small things quickly and change course when the evidence says so.
  • You can explain why this problem, and why you, in plain words.

The application

  1. What are you building, in two sentences?
  2. Why now?
  3. What is the hardest thing you have learned about your customers?
  4. What would you do with the stipend?
